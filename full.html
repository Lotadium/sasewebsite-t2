Who We Are

Our chapter is built on four core principles.

Mission

SASE changes lives by empowering Asian students to recognize their full potential and contribute to the global STEM landscape through community, mentorship, and professional development.

Vision

A future where Asian scientists and engineers are recognized as impactful leaders, driving innovation and creating lasting change across every field of technology and science.

Collaboration

We build an ecosystem of cooperation between students, professionals, and industry partners — hosting workshops, networking events, and real-world learning opportunities.

Leadership

SASE nurtures the leaders of tomorrow by connecting members with professionals who share their journeys and provide students with wisdom they can apply throughout their careers.
